Research

Interdisciplinary research with practical impact

My work connects material behaviour, advanced manufacturing, compliant mechanisms, robotic design, simulation and experimental validation. I am especially interested in materials and manufacturing approaches for sustainable smart devices, sensors, actuators and bioinspired robotic systems.

01

Magnetic Smart Materials

Functional Nd-Fe-B composites, magnetic actuation, locally tailored stiffness, flexible sensing and soft-device design.

02

Digital Manufacturing

Laser powder bed fusion, process optimisation, material qualification, rapid prototyping and design for advanced manufacturing.

03

Soft and Bioinspired Robotics

Robots inspired by butterflies, inchworms, rays and fish, developed for actuation, inspection, locomotion and assistive applications.

04

Sustainable Engineering

Rare-earth magnet recycling, circular-material strategies and resource-conscious design for functional composite systems.

05

Robotics and Automation

Industrial robot collision avoidance, trajectory optimisation, automated inspection and simulation-based engineering.

06

Data-Driven Engineering

Python, MATLAB, machine learning, modelling, visualisation and analysis for materials processing and robotic systems.

Selected projects

From material design to functional demonstrations

Magnetic composites by LPBF

Developing laser-powder-bed-fusion routes for magneto-active composites that link material composition, process parameters, geometry, stiffness and device function.

LPBFMagnetic compositesSmart actuators

Recycled Nd-Fe-B composites

Developing approaches for recycling rare-earth magnets and reusing recovered particles in degradable and functional magnetic materials for soft actuation and sensing.

Industrial robotics and inspection

Designed and validated collision-avoidance and trajectory-optimisation methods for industrial robots used in complex manufacturing and automated-inspection settings.
